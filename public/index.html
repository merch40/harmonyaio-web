How Harmony works
01

The parent connects the context.

Reason over the information available to the environment you are working in.

02

The gate checks the action.

Allow it, ask for review, or block it. Model confidence does not replace permission.

03

The agent carries out the work.

Execute authorized capabilities at the machine and report what happened.
